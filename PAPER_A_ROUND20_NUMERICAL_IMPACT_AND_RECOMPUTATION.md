# Round 20 — numerical impact and recomputation policy

## 1. The corrected problem

Every delivered dataset in rounds 1–19 was produced by an edge operator whose
electrical row was `q[ip]`, i.e. `φ(0,z) = 0`. The article's `eq:bc_edge` states
`D_x(0,z) = 0`. The corrected model is the charge-free edge; the grounded edge is
retained as a separate, documented configuration. `build_figure_data.plate()` now
defaults to `edge_electric="insulating"`; `Plate` itself defaults to `"ground"`,
so the round-19 baseline remains reproducible with a single keyword.

Nothing else was changed: no material constant, no constitutive term, no
discretisation parameter, no fitting.

## 5. What was **not** re-synchronised, and is therefore not claimed

The datasets in the last table changed, so *every* number in the article that is
read off those files changed or may have changed. This round verified and updated
the chain the article's headline claims rest on: `R_B` at the three reported
wavenumbers, the edge-plane share, the propagating/non-propagating partition, the
modal amplitudes and powers, and the certification of the maximum.

It did **not** re-derive, line by line, every secondary number drawn from
`fig02`, `fig06`, `fig07`, `fig08` and `fig10` (sweep endpoints, correlation
coefficients, per-thickness profiles, field-map statistics). Those files now hold
the corrected values, but the corresponding sentences in the article have not all
been re-checked against them. That is stated as an open item, not as a PASS:
**numeric re-synchronisation of the secondary numbers is UNVERIFIED.**

Edge-independent numbers (frequencies, group velocities, attenuation constants,
angles, dispersion) are unchanged both analytically and, where the containing file
is byte-identical, by file comparison.

## 6. Round-19 baseline preservation

The round-19 configuration is reproducible and tested:
`Plate(edge_electric='ground')` (still the model default),
`build_figure_data.plate(..., edge_electric='ground')`, and
`paper_a_study/figure_data/round19_baseline/` holds the two data files
(`modal_flux_audit.json`, `peak_search.json`) that `round19_tests.py` reads, so
that suite continues to verify the round-19 numbers it was written for while the
delivered data move to the corrected problem.
