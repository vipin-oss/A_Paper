\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
A ten-figure reproducible package for the fixed grounded ZnO plate model is complete. The figures cover dispersion, carrier parameters, thickness, frequency reflection, modal conversion, attenuation, H/$k_y$ coupling, boundary sensitivity, convergence and solved wave fields. Every new sweep is traceable to saved CSV/JSON/NPY data and to the scripts in \texttt{Paper\_A\_LaTeX/scripts/}. The original four historical figures remain separate. No new model, coupling mechanism or physical field is introduced.
