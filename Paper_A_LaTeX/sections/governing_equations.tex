\section{Governing equations and boundary conditions}
\label{sec:governing}
Let $x<0$ be the plate interior and $x=0$ be the free edge; $y$ is the propagation-plane coordinate with $k_y$ fixed. The phase ansatz is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ansatz}
\boldsymbol{u}(x,y,t)=\boldsymbol{U}(x)\exp\left[i(k_xx+k_yy-\omega t)\right],
\end{equation}
where $\boldsymbol{U}$ contains the three displacement components and the eliminated electrostatic/carrier fields. The momentum balance is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:momentum}
\rho\,\omega^{2}u_j+\frac{\partial}{\partial x_l}\sigma_{jl}
+\frac{\partial}{\partial x_l}\left(\ell^2\frac{\partial}{\partial x_m}\sigma_{jlm}\right)
=0,
\end{equation}
where $\sigma_{jlm}=\Delta n\,H_{jlm}\partial_m\varepsilon_{jl}$ in the minimal coupling. The electrostatic potential satisfies
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:electrostatic}
\kappa_{ij}\frac{\partial^2\phi}{\partial x_i\partial x_j}
+\frac{e}{\epsilon_0}\frac{\partial N}{\partial x_i}\frac{\partial\phi}{\partial x_i}
=0,
\end{equation}
and the carrier field obeys
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:carrier}
-i\omega N + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_i}\left(D_{ij}\frac{\partial N}{\partial x_j}+\mu N E_i\right)
=0.
\end{equation}
The boundary conditions are
\begin{subequations}
\label{eq:bcs}
\begin{align}
\sigma_{xx}(0,y)&=0,\\
\sigma_{xy}(0,y)&=0,\\
D_x(0,y)&=0 & \text{(grounded)}\quad\text{or}\quad \phi(0,y)=0 & \text{(open)}.
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
These equations are the fixed equations already implemented in \texttt{paper\_a\_study/model.py}. No new physical field, coupling mechanism or constitutive theory is introduced.
