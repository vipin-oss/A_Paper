\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}
This package reports a focused calculation of guided waves in a grounded 2-D plane-strain ZnO plate with a propagation-plane tangential component. The model retains the classical elastic, piezoelectric, dielectric, carrier-diffusion and flexoelectric sectors and adds a minimal three-displacement-component strain-gradient coupling through $H_{jkl}\partial_l\varepsilon_{jk}$. All numerical results below are computed by the scripts in \cref{sec:numerical_methods}; no experimental calibration is claimed for the synthetic $A$, $f_{3333}$, or $H$ inputs.

The objective is to quantify how the selected carrier parameters, thickness, gradient length, boundary condition and $k_y$ affect the dispersion, speeds, attenuation, backward-channel conversion, energy flux and numerical convergence. This is a figure-development and validation package, not a new model or literature review. The original four historical figures are retained separately in the repository and are not duplicated here.
