Paper A — note on the round-10 battery run (2026-11-07)
======================================================

`verification_log.txt` records the full battery as executed on 2026-11-07 during
round 10: 17 gates PASS, 1 gate FAIL, 1 SKIP (no TeX engine). The failing gate is
"claim registry and stale guard". This note records exactly what happened, because
the failure is a property of a regenerated record and not of the manuscript.

Sequence
--------
1. The battery was run on the round-10 tree. Several steps regenerate their own
   records; among them `peak_search.py`, which rewrote
   `paper_a_study/figure_data/peak_search.json`.
2. In the regenerated record the headline is unchanged to twelve digits
   (`K_at_max = 0.52752`, `R_B_at_max = 0.9467953677961777`, against the committed
   `0.9467953685099261`, a relative difference of 8e-13) and the classification is
   unchanged (`certified_local_maximum`). Two *spreads* moved:
     degree_spread      committed 4.973e-09 / 7.281e-09   regenerated 5.613e-09 / 6.220e-09
     quadrature_spread  committed 5.591e-09               regenerated 9.382e-10
3. `claim_audit.py` compares those two spreads with the registry values
   (5.0e-09 and 5.6e-09) at a 1 % relative tolerance and therefore failed, with
   9 source-agreement rows of which 7 passed. Nothing else failed, and every
   scientific gate passed — including the peak search itself, the identity audit
   and the matched controls.
4. Round 10 is a manuscript-only round, so the regenerated data records were
   restored to their committed state (`git checkout -- paper_a_study/figure_data/`)
   and the registry gate was re-run on the restored tree: PASS, 22 claims,
   45 tokens, 9 of 9 source-agreement rows.

Why the spreads move
--------------------
Both quantities are differences of nearly equal numbers (`R_B` at two Galerkin
degrees, `R_B` under two quadrature rules). Their value is at the round-off floor,
around 1e-9, while the underlying `R_B` values agree to about 1e-12 absolute
between runs. A 1e-12 perturbation of a 5e-9 difference is a 20 % change; the
registry tolerance of 1 % is therefore stricter than the quantity can support on a
different machine or a different BLAS.

Consequence for the manuscript
------------------------------
The two sentences that quote these spreads ("stable to 5.0e-09 between degrees 14
and 34", "5.6e-09 across four quadrature rules") are quoted at a precision the
re-run does not support. They remain true as order-of-magnitude statements and the
numbers are the ones in the committed record, so nothing was edited in round 10.
The correction belongs to round 11, where records and tolerances may be changed:

  ROUND11_SCIENTIFIC_OR_FIGURE_REVIEW_ITEM R10-5
    Report both spreads as bounds (for example "below 1e-08") rather than as three
    significant figures, re-derive `degree_stability_peak` and
    `quadrature_stability_peak` from a record produced on the delivery machine, and
    widen the registry tolerance for difference-of-two-nearly-equal-values claims.
